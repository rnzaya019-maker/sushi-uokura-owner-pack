3 · What we need from you

Your checklist

To launch

  • GoDaddy login for sushiuokura.com, or tell us who holds it (you, or the previous web company).
  • Opening hours for every day, including the lunch and dinner break and holidays.
  • All-you-can-eat prices for lunch and dinner, the time limit, and the table rules.
  • Google Business Profile access, so we can fix the name, hours, price and website link.

Please approve

  • The updated logo: house with a pagoda roof and a fish inside.
  • The restyled photos of the building, the sushi bar, the boat on a table and the entry. They are cleaned-up versions of your own photos.
  • One official name: "Sushi Uokura" is recommended, used the same everywhere (Google, Yelp, Apple Maps).

When you can

  • Who took the boat-and-plates photo on the black cloth, and may the site use it?
  • A real photo of the Uokura roll, your signature dish.
  • The menu: check the dishes listed, the market-price items, and the drinks. The site shows names only, no prices.
  • Gift cards and parties: how to buy, amounts, boat sizes, and an email address if you want one on the site.
  • Instagram and Facebook names, and who posts.

After launch

How changes work

Small edits

Hours, prices, a dish or a phone number: send us the change in a message.

New photos

Send the originals. We resize them so the site stays fast on phones.

Bigger ideas

Online ordering, an events page or a gift-card shop are separate pieces of work. The online ordering case explains the first one.

The website's prices and hours are placeholders until you confirm them. Nothing on the site is presented as final until it is checked with you.
