Should Uokura take online orders?

An honest look at demand, cost and staffing. The short version: fix the phone first, then try pickup-only through Clover.

Start with first-party pickup only, in capped hours. Skip DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub delivery for now.

The reviews do not show a shortage of demand. They show a phone that goes to voicemail and an order cancelled without a call. A pickup channel you control fixes that for the lowest cost per order.

1 · Demand

Real, but small, and mostly about reliability

We searched all 788 English-language Google reviews for takeout, to-go and delivery language. 19 reviews (2.4%) mention it. Satisfied takeout customers often do not mention the channel at all, so treat this as a floor.

“This is my go to Sushi Spot for take out… phone goes straight to voicemail.”5 stars · May 2024
“They cancelled my take out order bc it was ‘too long’… I found out from my door dasher.”1 star · October 2021

Uokura is the only restaurant in our 13-restaurant comparison, AYCE or popular tier, with no online ordering or reservation link. Okinawa sushi, also in Golden, has DoorDash, its own site and Google Reserve.

No option removes the packing work. Someone still has to bag the food while the sushi bar is running, which is the staffing limit the owner already named.

Net figures are before labor. Packing labor is about 1–2 extra hours a day at $16–18 an hour, roughly $480–1,080 a month under every option. Colorado's 2026 minimum wage is $15.16 an hour; Golden is not under Denver's higher rate.

Break-even for Clover pickup: the flat fee alone needs 3–5 orders a day. With labor included, about 9–10 orders a day, or two an hour in a set pickup window.

4 · Plan

What we recommend, and when to change it

Now

Make sure the phone is answered during service, and call customers before cancelling any order.

Next

Turn on Clover Online Ordering for pickup only, in a 2–3 hour window per shift.

Revisit

If pickup holds above 15–20 orders a day for a month, or counter staff are hired, add a marketplace listing in pickup-only mode.
